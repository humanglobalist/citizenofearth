# citizenofearth
🌍 Citizen of Earth

One planet. One humanity. One citizenship.

Citizen of Earth is a small project built around a simple idea: every human being belongs to Earth, regardless of nationality, ethnicity, background, or place of origin.

No borders. No passports. No complicated requirements.

Just three questions.

✦ The Declaration

To receive Earth citizenship, an individual must affirm:

1. Are you human?
2. Are you on Earth?
3. Do you love humans and Earth?

Answer yes to all three, and you qualify as a Citizen of Earth.

📜 Citizenship Certificate

Every new citizen can create and download a personalized certificate containing:

* Full name
* Unique 26-character citizenship ID
* Date of issuance
* Personal message to themselves and Earth
* Declaration of Earth citizenship

Each ID is randomly generated using the browser’s cryptographic random number generator.

🎯 Mission

The project aims to promote a simple message:

Humanity shares one home.

Nationality may divide us into different countries, but it does not change the fact that we inhabit the same planet.

Earth belongs to all of us.

No accounts, databases, or external APIs are required.

🔒 Privacy

The website generates citizenship certificates locally in the browser.

Names and personal messages are not submitted to a central server by the certificate-generation process.

Citizenship IDs are not registered in a central database.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Citizen of Earth is a symbolic citizenship project.

Its certificates are not government-issued identity documents, legal nationality, or official proof of citizenship.
